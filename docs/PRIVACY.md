# Privacy Policy — Onsen Planner

Effective date: 20 September 2026

Onsen Planner is developed by Nils Johansson. This policy describes the current application, with cloud synchronisation and new Pro purchases disabled.

## Planner data and storage

Your notes, expenses, trips, contacts, photos, holiday customisations and preferences are stored on your device. The developer does not operate a server that receives this information. The app contains no advertising, tracking or analytics service.

The app shares selected settings and cached information with its own widget through an Apple App Group on the same device. This does not share information with the developer or other applications.

Before a database upgrade, the app keeps a local recovery copy. If opening the database fails, the original files remain in place. Restoring a portable backup through the recovery screen creates a separate database.

## Permissions and Apple services

- **Contacts:** When you choose to import, update or export contacts, the app reads or writes the relevant information in Apple's Contacts database. Imported records are stored locally in your planner. Contacts you export may synchronise through the accounts you have configured in iOS.
- **Photos and camera:** Photos you select or capture for an entry are stored with your planner data. The app does not upload them to a developer-operated service.
- **Calendars:** The widget can read authorised calendar events for display. This does not grant the developer access to your calendars.
- **App Store:** The app uses Apple's StoreKit to check existing purchase entitlements. Apple may process account and transaction information under its own policies. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. New Pro purchases are disabled in this version.

You can manage permissions in iOS Settings. Other planner functions remain available if you decline a permission.

Onsen Planner's own cloud synchronisation is disabled. Apple's device backup, synced Contacts or Calendar accounts, and any cloud storage you choose in Files are separate services. Your use of those services is governed by their providers' policies, including [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Exports, sharing and external links

Backup files can contain private information, including contacts, notes and photos. Portable JSON backups are not password-protected by the app. Export and share them only to destinations you trust. A file saved to a cloud-backed location in Files may be uploaded by that provider.

PDF, CSV, contact and image exports go to the destination you select through the system sharing interface. Copies already saved or sent are managed separately from the app. Opening a website, map, telephone or email action passes the selected information to the relevant system application or service.

This policy is hosted on GitHub. Opening the policy visits GitHub and is subject to [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Deletion and recovery

You can delete individual planner records within the app. Local recovery checkpoints and previously exported backups may still contain earlier copies. Removing the app removes its app-container data; shared widget data, device backups and exports are subject to iOS and the storage services you use. Manage those copies separately in iOS Settings or Files.

The developer cannot recover your planner data from a server because no such copy is held. Keep a backup before removing the app or replacing your device.
